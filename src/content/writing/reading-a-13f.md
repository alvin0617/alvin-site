Institutional filings tell you *where* the conviction sits — but only if you
read them layer by layer instead of name by name.

## The layers

- Power and grid
- Memory and compute
- Optical and interconnect
- The neocloud layer on top

Each row in a 13F belongs to one of these. Sort by layer, not by ticker, and the
thesis reads itself.
